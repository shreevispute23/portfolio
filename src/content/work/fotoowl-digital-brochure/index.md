  A digital brochure for FotoOwl, an AI photo-sharing platform for photographers. The nine pages open on the brand
  promise, give one page each to AI Face Scan, Photo Selection, BEAM, Studio Manager and Branded Website, then cover
  the wider feature set, customer stories with platform figures, and the subscription plans.
objective: >-
  Communicate FotoOwl’s product features and benefits in one document. The pages are 9:16, the shape of a phone
  screen.
audience: Photographers considering FotoOwl.
communicates: What FotoOwl does for a photographer, one product per page, ending on proof and pricing.
channels: [Digital brochure (PDF)]
role: Designed the brochure, all nine pages.
process:
  - One product per page. Each page pairs a single headline in a yellow bar with a one-line benefit, a “Try It Now” prompt and a photo or product visual.
  - The FotoOwl wordmark, the headline treatment and the white-to-yellow ground repeat on every page, so the nine pages read as one piece.
  - The last two pages carry proof, customer videos and platform figures, then a plans table that ends on “Book a Live Demo”.
