overview: >-
  A single-page site written for startup founders. It opens with the positioning “India’s Innovation Company for
  Startups” and two calls to action, then works through short case stories on IP mistakes and wins, a featured patent
  with a labelled diagram, an index of industries linking to patent portfolios, six reasons to work with Technotreon,
  an FAQ and an intake form.
objective: Present Technotreon’s patent and innovation services to startup founders on a page that is also structured for search.
audience: Startup founders in India.
communicates: Why startups should plan IP early, and how Technotreon helps, from strategy to filing.
channels: [Website, Search (on-page SEO)]
role: Designed the page and built it as a working front-end prototype.
process:
  - An editorial type system of serif headlines, a clean sans for body text and monospaced labels, on warm paper with navy and gold accents.
  - Each case story follows the same pattern, what happened, a one-line lesson in italics, and what it means for founders.
  - Search basics are built in, a descriptive title and meta description, Open Graph tags, and Organization and FAQ structured data (JSON-LD).
  - The layout reflows to a single column on phones.
deliverables:
  - Responsive landing page (HTML, CSS and JavaScript prototype)
