overview: >-
  Instagram designs for FotoOwl, most of them from one Illustrator file of September posts. The static posts introduce
  E-Album Selling, photo selling, album selection and faster delivery, using FotoOwl yellow, a light grid background and
  product screens. The carousel changes tone: warm, filmic scenes contrast families gathered around a printed wedding
  album with guests today receiving their photos on their phones through AI face scan. A separate feature post, “Every
  event has a different story”, introduces a cover frame for each part of a wedding gallery.
objective: Introduce FotoOwl features on Instagram.
audience: Photographers who follow FotoOwl on Instagram.
communicates: One feature per post, such as E-Album Selling, photo selling and album selection. The carousel shows how photo delivery has changed.
channels: [Instagram feed (4:5)]
role: Designed the posts and carousels.
process:
  - The static posts share one system, a headline with yellow highlight bars, a supporting line, a product screen on a laptop or phone, and the FotoOwl wordmark top left.
  - The carousel moves from “Back then” scenes to present-day phones and lands on “The times changed. The memories didn’t.” with a Get Started button.
  - The event-frames post was produced in three layout variants.
tools: [Adobe Illustrator, Canva]
deliverables:
  - 8 static posts (4:5)
  - 8 carousel slides (4:5)
  - 1 feature post in 3 variants (4:5)
